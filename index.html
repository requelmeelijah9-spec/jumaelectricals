How it works

Four steps, no surprises on the invoice.

You call or message

Describe the problem or the job — a dead socket, a new extension, a solar setup you're considering. Maurice will tell you honestly whether it's a same-day fix or needs a site visit.

On-site evaluation

For anything beyond a quick repair, Maurice comes to look at the wiring, the board, and the space itself before recommending anything.

One flat-rate quote

You get a single price for the job, agreed before work starts. If something changes on-site, that's discussed with you first — not added to the bill after.

Work & warranty

Installation is completed and tested before Maurice leaves. Every job carries a workmanship warranty, so a callback for a fault is never a new invoice.
